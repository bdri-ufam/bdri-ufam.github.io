# Projects

## Projects coordinated by the group

- **Semantic Discovery and Explainability of Relationships in Data Lakes.** Integrated solutions for identifying and explaining semantic relationships in large data repositories, improving efficiency and interpretability. Funded by FAPEAM.
- **Integrating LLMs into Financial Recommendation Systems: Personalization and Bias Mitigation.** Methods that improve fairness and personalization in financial recommender systems by leveraging large language models. Funded by CNPq.
- **Neural Bond: Using Neural Language Models for Smart Social Media Engagement.** Neural language models to optimize user engagement and interaction on social media platforms. Funded by FAPEAM.
- **Methods and Techniques for Query Suggestion Systems.** Machine-learning methods for relevance ranking in query auto-completion, including the construction of labeled datasets and the design of ranking features. Funded by Jusbrasil.

## Research networks

- **INCT-TILDIAR.** National Institute of Science and Technology on Language Technologies for Responsible Artificial Intelligence, funded by CNPq (Call CNPq No. 58/2022). Altigran Soares da Silva is Vice-Coordinator of the institute, and BDRI is part of its network of participating groups.
- **[CIIA-Saúde](https://ciia-saude.dcc.ufmg.br/).** Center for Innovation in Artificial Intelligence for Health. Multidisciplinary project on AI solutions for diagnostics, treatment planning and healthcare management. Funded by FAPESP/MCTI, FAPEMIG and UNIMED-BH.
- **[INCT-IAIA](https://inct-iaia.vercel.app/).** National Institute of Science and Technology in Artificial Intelligence, funded by CNPq. The group participates in its steering committee.
