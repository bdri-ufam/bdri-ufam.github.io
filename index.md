# Databases and Information Retrieval Group

<p class="lead">BDRI is a research group at the Institute of Computing of the Federal University of Amazonas (UFAM), in Manaus, Brazil. The group works on data management, information retrieval and data mining, publishes regularly in national and international journals and conferences in these areas, and takes part in the Master's and PhD programs of the Graduate Program in Informatics (PPGI/UFAM).</p>

## Research topics

- **Data management:** keyword search over relational databases and document stores, schema matching, SQL query generation from text, knowledge graphs.
- **Information retrieval:** query processing and approximate prefix search, recommender systems, ranking and search in specialized domains.
- **Machine learning and language models:** information extraction, named entity recognition in low-resource domains, evaluation of language models for Portuguese.
- **Applications:** law, finance and health; the Web and social media as data environments.

The research lines registered with CNPq are *Document Management and Retrieval for the Web* and *Bioinformatics*.

## Technology transfer

According to its entry in the Directory of Research Groups, the group took part in the creation of technology-based companies and maintains agreements with information technology companies, especially those working on the Web.
