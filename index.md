# Databases and Information Retrieval Group

<p class="lead">BDRI is the research group on databases and information retrieval of the Institute of Computing of the Federal University of Amazonas (UFAM), in Manaus, Brazil. Founded in 2002, it works on data management, information retrieval and data mining, and, more recently, on machine learning and language models. The group publishes regularly in leading national and international journals and conferences and trains Master's and PhD students in the Graduate Program in Informatics (PPGI/UFAM).</p>

## Research topics

- **Data management:** keyword search over relational databases and document stores, schema matching, semantic join discovery in data lakes, SQL query generation from text, knowledge graphs.
- **Information retrieval:** query processing and approximate prefix search, query auto-completion, recommender systems, ranking and search in specialized domains.
- **Machine learning and language models:** information extraction, named entity recognition in low-resource domains, large language models for recommendation, text-to-SQL and evaluation of language models for Portuguese.
- **Applications:** law, finance and health; the Web and social media as data environments.

The research lines registered with CNPq are *Document Management and Retrieval for the Web* and *Bioinformatics*.

## Technology transfer and partnerships

Members of the group co-founded three technology companies: Akwan (acquired by Google in 2005), Neemu (acquired by Linx in 2015) and Teewa (acquired by JusBrasil in 2019). The group has maintained applied research partnerships with Jusbrasil since 2022, and its research is funded by CNPq, CAPES and FAPEAM (see [Projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }})).

## Recognition

A PhD dissertation supervised in the group received the SBC Thesis and Dissertation Award in 2013, together with an Honorable Mention in the CAPES Thesis Award. In 2015 the group's work was recognized with a Google Research Award in Latin America.
